- ~~When a mutation times out due to the backend being unavailable, once the backend becomes available again it does the mutation but doesn't update the local state.~~ **Fixed (2026-08-29).** The offline send now queues, redispatches after the backend returns, and converges local state — the root causes were silently-dropped realtime patches (raw ISO timestamps in trigger payloads the row decoders rejected) and the middleware subscribe race (snapshot computed before the subscriber registered). Covered by the "offline-sent message redispatched" reconnection assertion in the llm-chat browser suite.
